Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

This policy covers "HL Motion Studio Shorts Publisher", an internal tool of High Leverage Studios, and our TikTok and YouTube publishing integrations.

What the tool accesses

The tool signs in only to accounts owned by High Leverage Studios. With the permissions we grant it, it uploads our own videos and reads our own channels' public information and performance statistics (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, subscribers). It does not access other people's accounts, and it collects no personal data from viewers.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on our own production computer, in the operating system's protected credential store. Statistics are kept in our private production records. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deletion

To ask about or delete any data, write to hileveragestudios@gmail.com.
