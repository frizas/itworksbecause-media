Privacy Policy

It Works Because (@itworksbecause) and its publishing app "ItWorksBecause". Last updated: 3 November 2026.

What this app does

We publish short animated videos on our own Instagram account, read their statistics, and answer comments on them. When you comment the word a video asks for, we reply to your comment and send you one private message with the promised fact.

What we collect

For each comment we answer, we store the comment's ID, your Instagram username, the comment's text, the episode it belongs to, and whether we replied and sent the private message. We use this record only to avoid answering the same comment twice. We read our own videos' statistics as totals; they identify no one.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, or read your other messages or profile data. Instagram itself processes your comments and messages under Instagram's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep the record of an answered comment for up to 12 months, then delete it.

Deleting your data

To have your record deleted, send a message to @itworksbecause on Instagram with the word DELETE, or email hileveragestudios@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. Deleting your comment on Instagram also stops any further use of it by us.
